Oy (2024)
Designed by Kyeongho Park

Oy is an oyster shell–based buoy designed to replace plastic aquaculture buoys. At the end of its life, it sinks and becomes an artificial reef, supporting marine biodiversity instead of generating waste. Free of toxins, Oy creates a regenerative cycle that benefits both fisheries and the ocean ecosystem.
